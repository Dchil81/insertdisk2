Insert Disk 2 Support

Ten games from the family computer, on your iPhone. Something not working, or a question about the app? Email and you'll hear back.

Support email insertdisk2game@gmail.com

It helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version (Settings > General > About), and which game you were playing.

Common questions

Pager Pal's pages aren't reaching my phone.
Check Settings > Notifications > Insert Disk 2 and make sure notifications are allowed. In Pager Pal, open Options and make sure both Real Time and Notifications are checked. A newly hatched Pip pages within about 5 minutes; close the app or lock your phone and the page arrives as a notification.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Nothing is stored online, so progress doesn't sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes your saves.
I can't hear the games.
Game sounds follow the silent switch and mix with any music you're playing. Turn off silent mode, check that Sound is on in the game's Options menu, and tap the speaker in the desktop's taskbar if it shows a red "no" sign.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
PRIVACY.TXT - Insert Disk 2

Insert Disk 2 Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Insert Disk 2 collects no information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

  • No data collectedNothing about you or how you play is sent anywhere.
  • Saves stay on your deviceProgress and settings live only on your iPhone.
  • Works offlineThe app makes no network connections.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, Insert Disk 2 saves a small amount of game data on your device: levels you have unlocked, best scores and times, your settings, the names you type into Are We There Yet?, and your Pager Pal pet. This stays in the app's own storage on your device. The app also keeps a backup copy in the app's settings on the same device, so progress survives if iOS clears temporary storage.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Notifications

Pager Pal can send you notifications when your pet needs you. The app schedules them on your device; no server is involved and nothing leaves your device. You can turn them off in Pager Pal's Options menu or in the Settings app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not use advertising or analytics services, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share any data, because it has none to share.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Emails you send

If you email support, your message and email address are used only to answer you. They are never added to a mailing list or shared.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what the app stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Questions

Questions about privacy can be sent to insertdisk2game@gmail.com.
